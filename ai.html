AI & Creative Technology

Integration of AI throughout the creative process, from concept development and visual exploration to image generation, video, animation, and interactive experiences. A combination of AI, design, storytelling, and technology transforms ideas into distinctive visual worlds and innovative digital experiences.

Movie Trailer — After Effects

A cinematic trailer created in After Effects, combining motion, typography, rhythm, and sound to create a powerful visual experience. The project combines editing, animation, and visual effects to build tension, atmosphere, and anticipation — creating an immersive introduction to the film.

AI Concept Video - Coral Hotel

A concept video created with AI, transforming an organic material - coral - into an architectural hotel experience. The project combines AI-generated visuals, architecture, and motion to develop the material’s shapes, textures, and structure into a unique visual environment.

AI Music Video

A music video created and edited using AI, combining generated visuals, motion, and storytelling into a cohesive visual experience. The project uses AI throughout the creative process - from concept and visual generation to animation and final editing - creating a unique visual world that complements the music.

AI Commercial - Domino’s Pizza

A commercial created and edited using AI, combining AI-generated visuals with real brand elements from Domino’s Pizza. The project blends product imagery, recognizable brand identity, motion, and AI-generated environments to create a dynamic and engaging advertising experience.

AI Commercial - Leave Your Mark

A concept commercial for a footwear brand, created using AI around the idea of leaving a mark with every step. The project combines AI-generated visuals, movement, and storytelling to transform the footprint into a powerful visual symbol of identity, presence, and impact.
